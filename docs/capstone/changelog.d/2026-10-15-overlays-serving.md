# 2026-10-15 — Overlays and serving: one composition per apron window, global tokens, request-keyed ETags

- **One composition per apron window** (review 3.5-8, 3.6-3, 3.6-4, §4 item 3). `arda-blocks` composes every window grown by `COMPOSE_MARGIN` (8) squares and cuts it out (`crop.rs`, `Composed::cut`; logic/09 §window-crop): a border edge is decided with both of its squares in view, placements and lights sit on the world's 1/64-square lattice (refine snaps before its window filter), a neighbouring placement's footprint folds into the rules (canopy crowns now fold `lightly_obscured`). The server cuts a cell's or window's body and `/scene` from the apron block its image is drawn from (`Tactical::world_block_cut`, `Block::cut`, `BlockSource::check`), so a cell is composed once and body, scene and image agree; a window's body is its cells' bodies side by side. Query placements pick their family by world position when the layout has an origin. `/scene`, the folded rules and the dungeon scene use the image's render seed. NPC tokens are placed per building over the whole building as composed, in id order, workers at their workplace by day wherever it lies, never on a prop's square; a scene lists the tokens standing in it.
- **Seams**: a cell renders byte for byte as inside a window, plain and graded: the compositor keeps heights above sea level (not above the layout's lowest square) and the world grade fills water points from a lattice read four points wider with a fixed sand fallback (cell 529,812 differed by 5 and 2 bytes on recipe 9).
- **Serving**: a tile pyramid shares its render, which the render cache then lets go (3.6-5); `ETag`s name the request (server version, world identity with `society/`, `library_version`, route, path and query), are answered with 304 before any work, and every tactical body, scene, dungeon, overview and relief image carries `ETag` and `Cache-Control: no-cache` (3.6-11); refinement and the overlay layers run on the bounded rayon pool (3.6-12).
- **Scene** (3.6-6, 3.6-12): decked squares are walked at the deck's level; `deck_elevation_ft` is a typed `RulesCell` field (and `RulesCellDto`, additive; `ext.deck_elevation_ft` still written); bridge decks are levelled on the refined banks (`Terrain::play_ft`) and abutments ramp 5 ft a square to their banks; a diagonal between two raised squares is a climb; a parapet keeps its wall piece's light; locked doors are no spawn entrances.
- **Bake** (3.6-9): the splice follows the cell route's cut (border edges and the half-open position rule), so border edits are kept; posted items the cell cannot hold are counted in the new `ignored` field.
- **Overlays**: ferries span their open water shore to shore (`Terrain::open_water`, roped up to 256 squares) or land on each shore, also where a road stops short of the water or passes its record, and wet runs to 640 squares are crossed (3.5-9); fields read the ways' smooth centrelines and the refined rivers, mills look 28 squares beyond their cell (3.5-10); the ways' own dressing (ford posts, bank reeds, milestones) stands on bare ground beside their claims (3.5-11); farmhouses, barns, stables, mills, toll houses and waystations carry `BuildingId::rural` ids (3.5-12); cliffs and beaches are `Owner::Shore`, never fields or crofts (3.5-13); crossing runs are laid whole, a way fills water up to 2 ft dry (gravel bars, causeways; `drain()` takes effect), crofts make no ground, rules under a deck state the layout's water, a missing rug never takes the floor beyond a wall, fields and ways use `arda_ids::pmath` instead of libm (35 sites), and the fields plan floods only the fields within 40 squares of the window (3.5-14); plans near a window use `plan_radius_for` (OI-50).
- **Relief**: docks are drawn as decks; new release gate `tests/zoom_terrain.rs` (heights and woods, 3.6-10).
