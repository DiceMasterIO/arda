## 2026-10-15 - Settle at scale: default-size worlds settle, balanced realms, density as configured
- **arda-names** (OI-49): a crowded scope no longer refuses. Past the 48 plain draws, the ten distinguishing adjectives and 912 further draws, a place is named after a person (one founder's name or two founders' elements before the kind's head, 64 draws), and a name that still clashes takes the lowest Roman numeral the scope has not given it. `NameScope::place_name` returns a `Name`; `NamesError::ScopeExhausted` is gone. Names that resolved before are unchanged (logic/15 §name-unique).
- **arda-settle, population** (item 5): the population is density × land area again. logic/01 defines the configured density as the world's mean over its land; the carrying-land weights (arable ×2, habitable ×1, barren ×¼) made it people per carrying hectare and raised the legacy 250 × 500 km world from 617,437 to 958,828 people. Where people live still follows the land through site scores, refusals and farm-sized villages (logic/08 §settle-tiers).
- **arda-settle, realms** (OI-53): the territory is a balanced partition (`balance.rs`): each seat starts the cost flood with an offset, adjusted for up to 24 rounds so the realms of each group joined by land share its people (¾) and land (¼); a realm alone on an island keeps its island. When a group's largest realm still holds more than 2.5 times its smallest's people, the smallest's seat is dropped and the land partitioned again (at most three times). Invariant 5a is restated for larger worlds: bounds per landmass group, the fair-land bound MICRO's only (logic/08).
- **arda-settle, statistics** (OI-52): no libm behind any `society/` byte: `hypot` → `sqrt` of an exact sum of squares, `powi(2)` → a product, `num::ln`'s subnormal scale an exact constant; `tests/no_libm.rs` guards it. The overlay renderer still uses libm.
- **arda-server and arda-people** (OI-51): paged people queries read `World::generator` (16 cached skeletons), which now counts its builds (`World::skeletons_built`); admission charges an indexed `notables.json` as its header index at 3× and its lines at 2× (lines stay parsed for the life of the process), format-1 files whole at 2× as before.
