## 2026-10-16 - Relief tiles: no tidal-flat ghost patches over open water

- **arda-render (`atlas::fine::relief`)**: gallery 06's pale orange patches over open water by Taifithfold were the relief shader's tidal-flat tint (shore class 6, `0xa6a283`), not land use. Root cause: relief tiles drew sea through the pre-recipe-9 `shore_water`, which takes each class's share of the *classified* neighbours, so a sliver of a flat cell's bilinear weight read as full strength; and a flat tinted wherever the saved 100 m class grid held one, however far from land. On recipe-9 worlds relief sea now takes the same feathered `shore_water_soft` as the overview (class share is the plain bilinear weight), and the flat tint is scaled by its reach: whole within 100 m of dry saved ground and gone by 150 m, whole in water 0.3 m deep and gone by 1.2 m. Recipes 5-8 are unchanged byte for byte. The overview and area exports are untouched; they already feather by bilinear weight and a 0.5-4 m depth fade.
- **Docs**: logic/17 §render (relief sea tint).
- **Tests**: arda-render `atlas::fine::tests::{a_sliver_of_tidal_flat_class_does_not_tint_open_sea_at_relief_zoom, earlier_recipes_keep_their_relief_sea_tint, a_tidal_flat_out_over_open_water_is_not_tinted_but_one_by_the_shore_is, a_flat_by_the_shore_fades_out_once_the_water_is_a_metre_deep}`.
