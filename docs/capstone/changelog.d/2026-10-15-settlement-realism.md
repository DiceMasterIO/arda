# 2026-10-15 — Settlement realism: tested ports, measured flood heights, loop roads, emergent town sizes, a generation stamp

- **arda-settle, sites**: the stored HAND's "no downstream channel" placeholder is measured as the height above the nearest open water (sea level, a lake's spill level, a channel bed) before any rule reads it; land with no water anywhere is refused (`hand.rs`; review 3.3-3). `navigable` needs a reach at least 25 m wide carrying at least 28.7 m³/s (a 1 m deep channel by `d = 0.27·Q^0.39`); `harbour` needs a landing at most 5 m up on at most 6°; `navigable`, `harbour` and `estuary` reach only land within 10 m of the water they name (`field::near_low`; review 3.3-1). The grid loads discharge.
- **arda-settle, people and towns**: the population is density × carrying land (arable ×2, other habitable ×1, barren ×¼; review 3.3-13). Town count still follows the rank-size rule, but town sizes come from the hinterland within 24 km times the site advantage, raised to 5/2 (`townsize.rs`; review 3.3-12); villages and hamlets score ore, salt, fish, timber, passes and heights; town sites score ore and salt. The realm territory is drawn right after seat choice over the terrain cost surface; primacy and the city cap read it before land use; the capital holds at least `CITY_MIN` and a quarter of its realm; a third city's surplus goes to the realm's villages, towns, then seat (review 3.3-8, 3.3-9).
- **arda-settle, roads and water**: neighbouring towns more than 2× their straight distance apart by road get a direct highway when one exists within 1.6×; footpaths take their own line and are recorded only when they add cells; every per cent beyond a class's grade costs `25 % × excess²`, and each road records `steep_m` (review 3.3-6, 3.3-7). Channels under 2 m make no crossing; ferry legs are kept off `roads.bin` and road segments (review 3.3-14, 3.3-15). Fallow is `H(seed, "fallow", x, y)`. Border snapping keeps the raw partition when it would worsen it.
- **Generation stamp and plan fingerprint** (review 3.3-4, 3.3-5; section 4 item 4): `settlements.json` carries `stamp` (seed, code and world versions, recipe, a digest of `world.json`, and an id over every output byte). `arda society build` records the id as `settle_stamp` in `society.json` and `notables.json`, and a `plan_fingerprint` per settlement. `arda_people::World` refuses mismatched files with the file and the command to run (the server fails at startup); a plan drawn again with another fingerprint marks that settlement's notables stale (`/v1/settlements/{id}/npcs` answers an error). Response shapes unchanged.
- **arda-people and arda-npc** (review 3.3-10, 3.3-11): `notables.json` format 2 is one compact settlement a line behind a header index and is read lazily; `World::generator` keeps 16 owned population skeletons (`Generator::into_owned`), so commoner lookups do not rebuild their settlement. A plan housing under half its people falls back to the mix; a city's plan window grows with its people; off-plan cottages take the settlement's wealth.
