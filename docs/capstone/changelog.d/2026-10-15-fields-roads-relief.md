## 2026-10-15 - Straight strips, headlands, smooth switchbacks and moored boats

- **arda-fields**: open-field strips run straight on the furlong's bearing snapped to the nearest 45° of the square lattice, without the reverse S, so the tactical map draws their edges as straight lines of squares instead of stair-steps; a one-square grass balk separates them again (straight now), half a furlong's strips take its crop (was 72 %), and furlongs aim at 3.5 ha (was 6). A cell of field use that no worked cell touches along an edge (a lone cell, settle's diagonal chains along tracks) reads as wild ground, so it no longer makes a cell-shaped parcel (gallery 07's ghost rectangles; the mill compound itself still stands). Relief tiles draw the same strips and parcels (logic/17 §land-fields).
- **Forest margins** (V1's note on gallery 08 after its terrain merge): the fields' woodland fringe and a wood's own margin, and a town footprint's rough rim (`arda-town` `block::ground::croft`), lay grass and long meadow grass instead of `scrub`, whose art read as pale sandy rims between meadow and forest floor; their bushes and birch stay.
- **arda-blocks**: headlands. After the three layers compose, worked soil (`farmland`, `stubble`, `fallow`) of fields, crofts and towns is grass within 3 squares of a beach, cliff or open water and within 2 of built ground (buildings, yards, walls, squares), and worked ground the headlands leave under three squares across is grassed too; the grass is not difficult and drops `crop` and `furrow` (logic/09 §reservations; gallery 08's back gardens against the market houses and gallery 10's croft furrows on the beach). A town's boats are kept on water no layer claims.
- **arda-ways**: switchbacks are straight legs across the slope joined by circular hairpins of the class's tightest radius (carriageway and verges, at least 8 m), laid square to the piece's chord, with half legs leaving and rejoining the piece's line: no needle tips, no petal loops over tight corners, no kinks at piece joints (gallery 07's sawtooth). A piece up to 1.3 × its class grade climbs as it lies; waves reach at most 54 m either side (was 36) (logic/08 §roads).
- **arda-midzoom**: road surfaces are earth tones keeping two fifths of the ground's light, and hedges and walls are not drawn on a road's pixels or beside them, so a road no longer reads as a pale tube outlined by the hedge on its own cut (logic/17 §land-roads).
- **arda-town (dock dressing only)**: each dock moors a sailing boat off its seaward end (docks of 6 squares or more) and rowing boats about every 4 squares along both sides, 80 % of berths taken, on open plan water off every deck, building and other boat (`block/moorings.rs`; `prop.sailboat` falls back to `prop.rowboat`) (logic/10 §town-footprints; gallery 10 had one rowboat drawn on the next dock's deck).
- **Not fixed here (cross-stream)**: gallery 06's pale orange patches over open water by Taifithfold are not land use (they render identically on the world without `society/`): they are the Atlas relief shader's tidal-flat tint (`arda-render` `atlas::formed::shore_water`, shore class 6, `0xa6a283`) mixed bilinearly from the saved 100 m shore field over offshore sea cells; the fix belongs to `arda-render` (the overview and area-export stream).
