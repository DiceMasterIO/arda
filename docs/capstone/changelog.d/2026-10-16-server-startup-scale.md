## 2026-10-16 - Server startup at default size: no warm-up livelock, plans charged their grids, defaults admitted
- **arda-server (`refine_source`, `lib.rs`)**: `arda-server` never became ready on a default-size world (seed 42 recipe 9, 500 × 1000 km, settled and society built): 37 minutes on one core with no output. Root cause: the town-plan warm-up drew 32 plans at once over the whole world through the refine source, which opens a fresh `World` whenever a generation has touched 6 distinct areas; with 32 readers in different areas almost every read opened one, each re-verifying the 1.25 GB `terrain/fine.bin` (about 0.3 s, under the source's lock), so the readers livelocked (gdb: 31 threads in `BoundedSource::for_cell` waiting on the lock, one in BLAKE3; about 3 generations a second). Introduced with the bounded refine source (04bbe656); MICRO has 8 areas, so it never showed there. The warm-up now draws together only plans that read at most 8 areas between them (`WARM_AREAS`; `BoundedSource::warming` lifts the area bound to 8 for the warm-up and frees the larger generation after it), most populous first.
- **arda-people (`World::plan`, `warm_plans`)**: town plans are charged `plan_bytes` (their square grid, which JSON omits, plus twice their JSON) and the plan cache is bounded at 768 MiB as well as 64 plans, always keeping the newest. A default-size world's largest city holds a 509 MB plan, almost all of it grid; the 32 plans the old warm-up drew held 5.2 GB of grids, which the old twice-the-JSON charge (38 MB for the largest) did not count. The warm-up stops at half the count or bytes and sizes its batches with an 8 KiB-a-person estimate, so at default size it draws the largest city alone; `warm_plans` / `warm_towns` take the area bound instead of nothing (breaking signature change, server-only caller). `TERRAIN_MARGIN_M` names the 300 m terrain margin.
- **Admission and defaults**: the default budgets (15.6 GiB) left 0.4 GiB under the 16 GiB ceiling, so even without the hang a default-size world (about 1 GiB of society files, shore layer and library) was refused. Admission now charges the plan cache's 768 MiB plus the largest plan drawn; `--max-connections` 64 → 32, `--area-cache-mib` 384 → 256, `--fine-cache-mib` 256 → 128, overview PNG and tile caches 256 → 128 MiB each. Tactical caches are unchanged. Seed 42 default size admits 15,949 of 16,384 MiB.
- **Measured** (release, default config, placeholder library, 32 cores): default size ready in 13.0 s (15.3 s on a second copy), peak RSS 2.5 GiB, from never ready; MICRO ready in 1.9 s (2.0 s before), `server_open` gate 1826 ms (1918 ms before). Response bytes are unchanged (caches and warm-up only).
