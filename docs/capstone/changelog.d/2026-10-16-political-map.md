## 2026-10-16 - Political map: realm edge bands, one colour per realm, no hamlets at world scale

- **arda-settle (`render`)**: the maintainer's chosen political style (variant A1 of `explore/political-variants`) is the default page of `arda-settle render`: each realm's colour as an inner band strongest at its land border and fading inward over `11 · s` px, the border as a dark dash-dot line over a pale halo drawn over the roads, no area fill, and ivory roads in a thin sepia casing (width by class; tracks dashed, footpaths dotted over a faint halo). `--style classic` keeps the earlier tinted page. Realm colours come from `render::palette::Palette`: exactly one hue per realm, evenly spaced round OKLab less the olive and green of lowland relief, three lightness steps, assigned most-constrained first and refined by swaps so neighbours stand apart; no colour is reused (the prototype's ten colours repeated with eleven realms). The band is a pure function of pixel position (distance to the smoothed border lines through a bucket index, realm of the cell), so strips and tiles join. The legend lists only what the page draws, paints its road swatches with the map's road painter and its border swatch with the map's band and line constants; the title block wraps the realm list (kept from the prototype).
- **World scale**: hamlets, and every road built from a hamlet (all tracks), are drawn only at 30 m/px or finer (`render::scale::HAMLET_MAX_M`, the tracks' own threshold). The offline page, overview exports with the society layer, `/v1/overview.png` with an overlay and all overview tiles of MICRO and default-size worlds now show cities, towns and villages only (content change on those routes); area exports and relief tiles keep every hamlet.
- **Exports and server**: `arda export --overview --society political` and `?overlay=political` on `/v1/overview.png` and `/v1/tiles/overview/…` paint the political layer (additive value; renders, pyramid bases and tiles are cached per overlay). The default `society` overlay keeps its classic roads. `--society political` on an area export is refused. `/v1/realms` gains `colour` (`#rrggbb`) per realm (additive optional field; schema baseline unchanged, `bindings/schema/RealmList.json` regenerated).
