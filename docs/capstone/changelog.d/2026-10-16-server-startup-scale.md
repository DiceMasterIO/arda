## 2026-10-16 - Server at default size: starts in 13 s, no refine-source thrash, plan memory bounded, defaults admitted
- **Startup hang (arda-server)**: `arda-server` never became ready on a default-size world (seed 42 recipe 9, 500 × 1000 km, settled and society built): 37 minutes on one core with no output. Root cause: the town-plan warm-up drew 32 plans at once over the whole world through the refine source, which opened a fresh `World` whenever a generation had touched 6 distinct areas; with 32 readers in different areas almost every read opened one, each re-verifying the 1.25 GB `terrain/fine.bin` (about 0.3 s, under the source's lock), so the readers livelocked (gdb: 31 threads in `BoundedSource::for_cell` waiting on the lock, one in BLAKE3; about 3 generations a second). Introduced with the bounded refine source (04bbe656); MICRO has 8 areas, so it never showed there.
- **Refine source (arda-server `refine_source`, arda-refine `WorldSource::sibling`)**: generations are replaced by 8 kept decoded areas, least recently used evicted, each read through its own `World` (a manifest load) and a `WorldSource::sibling` of the base source that shares its fine-terrain reader, fine-height memo and shore layer, verified once at open. Opening an area never re-reads or re-hashes the fine terrain and happens outside the source's lock; readers spread over more areas than are kept only decode areas again. Admission: 8 kept areas plus as many evicted ones still being read and one fine memo (was two generations of 6 with a memo each); the shore layer is held twice, not three times. `RefineError::NotSibling` is new.
- **Town plans (arda-people `World::plan`, `warm_plans`)**: plans are charged `plan_bytes` (their square grid, which JSON omits, plus twice their JSON). A default-size world's largest city holds a 509 MB plan, almost all of it grid; the 32 plans the old warm-up drew held 5.2 GB of grids, which the old twice-the-JSON charge (38 MB for the largest) did not count. One 768 MiB budget (`PLAN_CACHE_BYTES`) now holds the cached plans (at most 64) and the plans being drawn: a draw reserves `plan_estimate` (8 KiB a person plus 16 MiB; across 80 default-size plans the largest was 81 % of its estimate, and MICRO plans are checked by a test) before drawing, evicting cached plans first, and otherwise waits; a plan over the budget is drawn alone and the newest plan is always kept. Draws run in a rayon pool of their own, entered from a plain thread, so a waiting draw is never stacked on the draw it waits for. The warm-up draws most populous first, in batches whose plans read at most 8 areas between them and whose estimates fit the budget, until the cache holds half its count or bytes; `warm_plans` / `warm_towns` take the area bound (breaking signature change, server-only caller). `TERRAIN_MARGIN_M` names the 300 m terrain margin.
- **Admission and defaults**: the default budgets (15.6 GiB) left 0.4 GiB under the 16 GiB ceiling, so even without the hang a default-size world (about 1 GiB of society files, shore layer and library) was refused. Admission charges the 768 MiB plan budget plus the largest plan drawn; `--max-connections` 64 → 32, `--area-cache-mib` 384 → 256, `--fine-cache-mib` 256 → 128, overview PNG and tile caches 256 → 128 MiB each (maintainer-accepted). Tactical caches are unchanged. Seed 42 default size admits 15,854 of 16,384 MiB.
- **Measured** (release, default config, placeholder library, 32 cores): default size ready in 12.9 s, peak RSS 2.6 GiB, from never ready; 16 concurrent cold tactical cells in 16 areas (8 cities) in 101 s at 3.9 GiB peak, against 76 s at 6.3 GiB with plan draws unbounded, bodies byte-identical; MICRO ready in 1.9 s (2.0 s before). Response bytes are unchanged (caches, warm-up and scheduling only; contract kit unblessed).
