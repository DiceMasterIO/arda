# 2026-10-15 — Town plans survive composition: docks, whole buildings, level floors, streets at town entries

- **arda-town**: a dock's squares are owned by the town block (the grid keeps them water; review 3.5-2), so docks are drawn as decks over water with `deck: true` rules and their building id. A deck's rules cell states the deck and no `water_depth_ft`, so the water beneath is the layers' below.
- **arda-people**: a town plan reads the refined water square for square: lake and sea shores from `arda_refine::region::WaterRegion::standing` (the blocks' shore rule) instead of whole 100 m open-water cells (review 3.3-2), with the refined rivers as before; marsh pools stay made ground. No building is planned on water a composed block refuses to dry (review 3.5-3). Plans cost 10–25 % more to draw (MICRO 42, all 658: 22.2 s against 18.0 s).
- **arda-refine**: new `heights::Heights`, the block's `elevation_ft` at any global square (the square's own block context and channel pieces, evaluated at that one square), cached per cell.
- **arda-blocks**: `OverlayLayer::elevation` is a per-square mask: a layer states the elevation only of the squares whose height it knows (the rules contract). The town layer (`society::levels`) levels each walled building's floor to the refined ground outside its street door, the market square and greens to the ground at the market, baileys to the keep's feature point, and raises the curtain's wall-walk 20 ft over the refined ground; levels are read at global squares, so a building straddling two windows is levelled alike (review 3.5-4). Town decks keep the refined water and ground beneath; a dock carries its deck level in `ext.deck_elevation_ft` (additive, as ways' bridges). New `overlays::compose_over` judges the water a claim may not dry against the shallower of the composed layout and the refined terrain, so a ford's wading depth laid through a marsh pool no longer unbuilds a town building that outranks the road (`compose` keeps its signature). `Streets::replace` gives a road square up only where the town lays public street ground; elsewhere in the reach the road stays road (review 3.5-6). New `Streets::within`.
- **Docs**: logic/09 §elevation and §reservations; logic/10 §town-footprint, §town-rivers, §town-clip, Invariant 13; logic/12 §scene-sidecar; logic/17 §land-roads (relief skips only the road squares a street replaces, as the tactical map does).
- **Why**: review 3.5-2, 3.5-3, 3.5-4, 3.5-6 (High), with 3.3-2. Docks were sited on plan water the town never owned; the planner saw whole-cell water while the blocks drew bent shores, so buildings lost squares to refined water while their walls stayed; every layer passed `elevation: false`, so floors followed the 5-ft contours; the street replacement freed every road square in the reach, street or not.
- **Before → after (MICRO 42 recipe 7, the stamped arda-people fixture; every plan, 658 plans, 3,362 cells, `tests/tactical_drops.rs` `measure_every_plan`)**: docks drawn 0/216 → 216/217 (the other lies under a neighbouring town's bridge, which claims it first); walled buildings losing squares 342/26,312 (182 homes) → 0/26,279; buildings with stepped floors 15,305/26,312 (every one of the 4,904 on ground rising ≥ 10 ft) → 0; wall-walk squares 20 ft over the ground 0/24,223 → 24,234/24,234; road squares in a town's reach that became crofts, fields or bare ground 87,276/322,392 (27 %) → 0/320,529.
