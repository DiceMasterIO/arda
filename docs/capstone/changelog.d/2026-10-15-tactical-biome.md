## 2026-10-15 - One climate-to-biome module; tactical ground, snow, rivers and frames agree with the world
- **arda-core `biome`** (new module, review §4 item 6): the evaporation demand (300 mm + 35 mm/°C), the tree line (`-1 → +4 °C`) and snow line (`-4 → -7 °C`) with a seasonal shift of 0.4 °C per degree poleward of 45° (at most 10 °C), the bare-rock exposure with vegetation as a co-equal input (open ground half rock at 35°, a closed canopy holds to 45°, frost above the tree line bares 8° earlier), the world's cover thresholds, the latitude frame (`Manifest::biome_frame`) and the aspect encoding (whole degrees, `0` flat, `360` north; hemisphere-aware sun). It lives in `arda-core` because every consumer already depends on it and the aspect encoding is part of the cell format. World ecology, the refiner, the Atlas and relief shader and the server's snow proxy read it; settle's orchard test reads its aspect rule (one line).
- **World generation, recipe 9 only** (recipes 4–8 and legacy replay their bytes, `tests/golden_recipes.rs`, `tests/golden_world.rs`): slope, aspect and wetness read a three-cell halo of the neighbouring areas (`area::fields9`, `area_output::compose_recipe`; review 3.2-5): the slope stays the steepest descent to a neighbour, now across the edge (Horn's mean gradient, tried first, ran below every threshold the world is set on and pushed settle's rank-size fit out of range); the aspect is Horn's whole-degree bearing, flat unambiguous (3.2-7); wetness is the topographic index on continuous logarithms smoothed over 5 × 5 land cells (3.2-16); ecology (`orchestrator::ecology9`) uses the shared tree line, so boreal forest grows below -4 °C at high latitude, puts ice under the full snow line at any height (3.2-8), draws rock and canopy from the shared exposure (3.4-2), shades by hemisphere (3.2-7) and reads rotated, phase-offset soil noise (3.2-11). Structural relief keeps its lattices: only legacy terrain reads it (logic/02 §recipe 9).
- **Atlas and relief, recipe 9**: canopy and snow follow the shared lines at each row's latitude, and the sunny side is the one facing the equator (`AtlasTerrain::with_frame`). Older recipes keep their pinned look, which took the gradient's southward component, the north-facing side, for the sunny one. The server's snow proxy uses the recipe-9 line at the cell's latitude.
- **arda-refine**: snow at a square is the world's snow line with the Atlas's sun, hollow and ridge corrections, weighted as odds against the other ground so the share of squares under snow follows the world's fraction (review 3.4-9); the tree line, evaporation demand and aridity are the shared ones; shade and sun follow the hemisphere (3.2-7). Rock, scree, cliff, strata and outcrops follow the shared exposure under the world's forest density, scree and strata lead the slope only on open ground, roots halve the detail roughness's slope term, trunks stand wherever the ground is not bare rock (3.4-2). Block frames are solved so the WFC can complete them: corner stars, edge passes that keep shared inner corners completable, and bent masks where fixed features cannot meet (3.4-5). Rivers run through their formed thalwegs (`water.bin` layout 3) and through via cells on diagonal steps (3.4-7); banks are shaped 8 squares out (was 20), cut at most 2.5 m, the water surface is the lowest ground at and beside the banks less the incision, and dry ground within two squares of any channel's water stays above it (3.4-8). Placements snap to 1/64 square before rules read them; blocks gather three cell rings so halo trails agree; water depth in feet rounds; `pools.rs` uses no `hypot` (3.4-10). New required `Source::biome_frame` and `Source::thalweg` (forwarded by `arda-people` `SharedSource` and the server's `BoundedSource`).
